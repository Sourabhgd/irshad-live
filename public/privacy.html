Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Website: irshad.live ("इरशाद...")

At irshad.live, accessible at https://irshad.live, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by irshad.live and how we use it.

When you offer an image to "इरशाद...", the image is processed in real time solely to generate reflective Urdu and Hindi verses and couplets. 3. Log Files & Analytics

irshad.live follows a standard procedure of using log files. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California (CCPA), you have rights regarding your personal data. You may manage cookie preferences at any time via the consent notice displayed upon visiting the website.
